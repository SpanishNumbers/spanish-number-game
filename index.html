16 to 29 are one word each: dieciséis, veintidós, veintinueve. From 31 on, “y” goes only between the tens and the ones: treinta y uno, but ciento uno and mil dos.

Exactly 100 is cien; 101 to 199 use ciento. Watch the three odd hundreds: quinientos (500), setecientos (700), novecientos (900).

Uno shortens to un before a masculine noun (veintiún pesos), and hundreds turn feminine before feminine nouns (trescientas vacas, doscientas personas).
